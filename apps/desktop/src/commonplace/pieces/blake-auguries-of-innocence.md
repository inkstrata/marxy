# Auguries of Innocence

To see a World in a Grain of Sand\
And a Heaven in a Wild Flower\
Hold Infinity in the palm of your hand\
And Eternity in an hour

---

William Blake (1757–1827). The opening quatrain of "Auguries of Innocence," copied out by Blake about 1803 in the notebook now called the Pickering Manuscript, and unpublished in his lifetime. It follows the manuscript, which has no punctuation; the first printing, in Gilchrist's *Life of William Blake* (1863), was retouched by D. G. Rossetti.
