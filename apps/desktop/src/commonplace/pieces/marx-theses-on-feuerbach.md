# Thesen über Feuerbach

## Deutsch

2\. Die Frage, ob dem menschlichen Denken gegenständliche Wahrheit zukomme, ist keine Frage der Theorie, sondern eine praktische Frage. In der Praxis muss der Mensch die Wahrheit, d. h. die Wirklichkeit und Macht, die Diesseitigkeit seines Denkens beweisen. Der Streit über die Wirklichkeit oder Nichtwirklichkeit eines Denkens, das sich von der Praxis isolirt, ist eine rein scholastische Frage.

⋮

11\. Die Philosophen haben die Welt nur verschieden *interpretirt*; es kommt aber darauf an, sie zu *verändern*.

## English

2\. The question whether objective truth belongs to human thinking is not a question of theory but a practical question. In practice man must prove the truth, that is, the reality and power, the this-sidedness of his thinking. The dispute over the reality or unreality of a thinking that isolates itself from practice is a purely scholastic question.

⋮

11\. The philosophers have only *interpreted* the world in various ways; the point, however, is to *change* it.

---

Karl Marx (1818–1883), two of the eleven theses he wrote in Brussels in the spring of 1845. They were first printed by Friedrich Engels, lightly edited, as an appendix to his *Ludwig Feuerbach* (Stuttgart: J. H. W. Dietz, 1888), whose text and spelling this follows; its spaced-out type is set in italics. Thesis 11 is carved on Marx's grave in Highgate.

It is worth saying, in a reader, that the eleventh thesis is about what comes after reading.
