# Omnia praeclara

## Latina

Si jam via quam ad hæc ducere ostendi, perardua videatur, inveniri tamen potest. Et sane arduum debet esse quod adeo raro reperitur. Qui enim posset fieri si salus in promptu esset et sine magno labore reperiri posset ut ab omnibus fere negligeretur? Sed omnia præclara tam difficilia quam rara sunt.

## English

If the way I have shown to lead here now seems very hard, it can still be found. And indeed it must be hard, since it is so seldom discovered. For how could it be, if salvation were ready to hand and could be found without great labour, that it should be neglected by almost everyone? But all things excellent are as difficult as they are rare.
