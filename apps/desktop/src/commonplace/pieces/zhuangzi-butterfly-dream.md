# 莊周夢蝶

## 中文

昔者莊周夢爲胡蝶，栩栩然胡蝶也，自喻適志與，不知周也。俄然覺，則蘧蘧然周也。不知周之夢爲胡蝶與？胡蝶之夢爲周與？周與胡蝶，則必有分矣，此之謂物化。

## English

Once Zhuang Zhou dreamed he was a butterfly, a butterfly fluttering here and there, happy with itself and doing as it pleased. It did not know it was Zhou. Suddenly he woke, and there he was, solidly Zhou. He did not know whether he was Zhou who had dreamed he was a butterfly, or a butterfly dreaming it was Zhou. Between Zhou and a butterfly there must be some difference. This is what is called the transformation of things.

---

Zhuangzi (Zhuang Zhou, 4th century BCE), the last passage of the second chapter of the book that bears his name, "On the Equality of Things," in the received text of Guo Xiang (d. 312).
