# Fragments

(1) It is wise to hearken, not to me, but to my Word, and to confess that all things are one.

(10) Nature loves to hide.

(13) The things that can be seen, heard, and learned are what I prize the most.

(41, 42) You cannot step twice into the same rivers; for fresh waters are ever flowing in upon you.

(95) The waking have one common world, but the sleeping turn aside each into a world of his own.

---

Heraclitus of Ephesus (fl. c. 500 BCE). Five of the fragments, in John Burnet's translation from *Early Greek Philosophy* (2nd ed., London: A. and C. Black, 1908), numbered as in Ingram Bywater's edition of 1877. "Word" is Burnet's rendering of *logos*.
