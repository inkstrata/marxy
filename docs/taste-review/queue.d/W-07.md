# W-07 — the sidebar

Unfold the workspace (Mod+\), open a dozen documents, pin one (Mod+. in the palette). Judge by looking:

- Two sections, Recent and "Pinned · Changed since you read": is one heading for two lists right, or should they be two headings? Layout is this story's; W-04 owns the look.
- A row is glyph, title, quiet age; a changed file's age is in the accent. Enough to tell it from a pinned one, or does it want the palette's dot?
- Fold a section: the header keeps a quiet count. Option-click folds or opens both.
- At `chrome_size = 26`: do the rows still fit the 252 px column?
- The reading column must not move when the sidebar is docked or undocked.
