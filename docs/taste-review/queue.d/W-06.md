# W-06 — the tab row

Unfold the workspace (Mod+\), open three or four documents in turn. Judge by looking:

- The row: glyph, name and unsaved dot only; the active tab (accent line, wash) against a tab the other pane shows (quiet secondary line). Layout is this story's; W-04 owns the look.
- The 34 px band appearing at the second tab and going at the first: does the text jumping 34 px down feel right, or should the band stay as an empty strip?
- At `chrome_size = 26` and a narrow window: the row scrolling with faded edges, the active tab kept in view.
- Click a tab over a document with unsaved edits: Save, Discard, Dismiss is Chrome-unlike; the author ruled it (a background tab keeps no edits, W-06.1 would).
