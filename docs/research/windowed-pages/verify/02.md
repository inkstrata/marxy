## Corrections for the lead

1. TIR-07: replace the "Enter step" sentence with the real ranges (WebKit 0 to 5 ms in prose up to 2 MB, 15 to 41 ms in `md-technical`; Chromium 0.4 to 6.2 ms in prose, 9 to 13 ms in `md-technical`). State that Chromium's starting match is within a few matches of off at 1 MB and up, not identical.
2. TIR-02: reword the `md-technical` clause. The counter is `.marxy-set` elements, not paragraphs (2,679 set against 2,700 without the rule, 2,300 `p`). Tag the cause "because the Range read is empty" [likely]. In the TL;DR and the Recommendation, "never recovers because `typeset_done` still fires and scrolling does not queue it" is supported by the unchanged count, and `index.ts` agrees (the queue empties and the observer is dropped regardless of success). Say it is read from the code, not traced. TIR-07's WebKit find cause is also [likely].
3. TIR-04 and the Recommendation: add the Chromium `md-technical` 1 MB result (5.3 s to 22.9 s task time, 4.3x). It is larger than the 1.3 to 2x quoted. The "35 minutes" figure has no timing in the output, so say "about 35 minutes by file times".
4. TIR-05: change "median of 3" to the actual run counts (2 at 1 MB, 1 at 2 MB and 4.9 MB). "Costs the same" should read "no lower", since several rule cells are higher.
5. TIR-08: table real sum is 259,500, not 258,000. Check the p90 of 2.03 (WebKit gives 1.97).
6. Setup says "median of 3 at 0.25 MB" and the 4.9 MB WebKit/Chromium rows are single runs. The 4.9 MB WebKit 6,972 ms find time, the 4.9 MB scroll comparisons and the 2 MB slowdowns are each one run, so they are weak evidence.
7. No [absence] findings. The chapter has no finding whose title claims something does not exist.
