# Replan, 2026-10-11: from parts to surfaces

**In short.** Since 2026-10-09, 77 pull requests merged, all on Sonnet, and the app a reader opens has changed
little. Most of the direction's merged work (kinds, the clipboard API, the transform library, the query language,
capture rules, the measure, `chrome_size`) has **no surface**: nothing calls it, or only the palette reaches it.
The look is held for the author's taste, and most of the rest has not been in front of the author at all. The next
waves therefore **wire what exists into the window** before building more parts: the kind at open (K-05a), Copy as
and paste (J-04, J-05), the workspace shell and its keys (W-02, W-19), then the toolbar that gives the transforms,
Copy and Export a door (W-05, W-21). One taste walk puts today's work in front of the author in a single sitting.

Evidence: a headless survey of `main` at `7b543931` (Playwright WebKit over the memory shell; recipe in
`roadmap-2026-10/harness-map.md`), CI history since 2026-10-09, the ledger, and the cards.

## Trajectory

- **Order.** The first milestone (v0.6.0: H, K-01 to K-11, K-20, J-01 to J-05) was being built bottom-up: every
  record and core API landed, almost no door. K-07 needs W-05's toolbar, so it moves to v0.7.0 with the workspace.
  From here a story that adds a core API names its caller, in the same story or the next queued one.
- **Taste.** The taste queue holds 16 unreviewed rows (A-02 to MARXY-346), and the direction's visible stories
  (H-03.1, K-18, D-panes, E-verbs, V-01) never reached the author in the app. **X-11, the taste walk**, captures them
  as before-and-after pages in one folder, and the author rules in one sitting. H-04 (#551) is its first page.
- **Review depth.** It matched risk: D-10, D-11, V-07, B-24, V-01 got adversarial reviews and each found a real
  hole; trivial and docs changes got lead reviews. Keep it; F-33 makes the adversarial ones cheaper.
- **Cost.** About 38 merged stories a day on Sonnet, two to three implementors and one or two reviewers. There is no
  token meter, so cost per story is not measured; the proxy is returns (about a third of stories returned once).
  Keep Sonnet; Opus only where a card's rule names it after a Sonnet failure.
