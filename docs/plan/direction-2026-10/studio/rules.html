2 · The Privacy page

Shown while history is not off: CLIPBOARD_HISTORY_PRIVACY_LINE, then, in keep, the folder.

Privacy · what Marxy keeps

Marxy keeps what you copy in Marxy, and what you ask it to keep, on this Mac, in memory until you quit or, if you choose to keep history between launches, as plain files in Marxy's data folder. It reads what other apps copy only when you paste or ask it to, and sends nothing anywhere.
