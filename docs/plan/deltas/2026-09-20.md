## The thing the trigger didn't know to ask about

Parking MARXY-78 should have been enough to free MARXY-138 in `ready.mjs`. It wasn't — running
`node orchestration/ready.mjs` after the park still showed **MARXY-138 blocked by paths**, twice,
for two different and unrelated reasons, neither of which is MARXY-78:

- **MARXY-28** (`KaTeX on first use, on the grid`) has had `Paths` of a bare `packages/core/src/render,
  apps/desktop` since it was written — the entire app tree — while its own task card
  (`docs/plan/tasks/MARXY-28.md`) only ever touches `apps/desktop/src/render/math.ts` (new) and a
  wiring call in `apps/desktop/src/app.ts`. As long as MARXY-78 (ops) sat `in_progress` on
  `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src`, that wildcard was itself blocked and nobody noticed it would also
  block every other `apps/desktop` story the moment the ops story moved. Parking 78 surfaced it
  immediately: `ready.mjs`'s product-over-ops sort still processes stories within a phase in CSV
  order, and MARXY-28 sits earlier in the file than MARXY-138, so it was picked first and froze
  MARXY-138 out on the shared `apps/desktop` prefix.
  **Fix:** narrowed MARXY-28's `Paths` to the files its card actually names
  (`packages/core/src/render`, `apps/desktop/src/render/math.ts`, `apps/desktop/src/app.ts`,
  `apps/desktop/package.json`, `apps/desktop/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`), and added MARXY-138 as a
  formal dependency (CSV Description, `deps.json`, and the card's front matter and prose) because
  both stories still need to add a call in the same `app.ts` — a real conflict, not an artifact of
  the wildcard, and MARXY-138 is the standing next-in-line story.
- **MARXY-30** (screenshot baselines) and **MARXY-138** both list `fixtures/baselines` in `Paths`
  — the exact pairing the trigger brief already named ("do not dispatch 30 and 138 together...
  hold 30 until 138 is in_review or done"), but that rule lived only in prose. `ready.mjs` doesn't
  read prose: with both eligible, MARXY-30 sits earlier in the CSV than MARXY-138 and would be
  picked first, which is the opposite of the stated order. **Fix:** added MARXY-138 (and,
  while there, MARXY-137, which the story already assumes since it gates the baselines MARXY-137
  recaptures) as formal dependencies of MARXY-30 in `deps.json` and the task card. This is stricter
  than "in_review" — it requires MARXY-138 `done` — which is a safe direction to round in given
  the standing instruction, and is named here per the "say why if you change the order" rule: the
  order itself is unchanged, only made mechanical.

Neither of these was visible from the trigger's board-facts sheet, which only looked at MARXY-78
vs MARXY-138. Both are now checked by `node --test orchestration/phases.test.mjs` (the committed
board's phase/dependency invariants, 21/21 green) and confirmed live by `ready.mjs`.

## Operational note: the live loop and this checkout

Mid-pass, `orchestration/loop.sh --minimal` was found running live in this exact primary checkout
(started 18:15 PDT, predating this session). Its `cycle.mjs` fetched, fast-forwarded `main` to
`origin/main` (landing `fc02a21`/#104 and, moments later, `a8b0a0e` MARXY-70), and — going by the
reflog (`reset: moving to HEAD` immediately before the fast-forward) — the working tree was
briefly hard-reset, which discarded this pass's uncommitted edits to
`docs/plan/jira-issues.csv`, `orchestration/deps.json` and the task cards for one cycle before
they were re-applied and re-verified. `orchestration/state.json` (gitignored) and other untracked
files were unaffected, which is why the MARXY-78/MARXY-126 board edits survived throughout.
**The loop was stopped (`kill -9`) to finish this pass safely** and has not been restarted. Two
things for whoever restarts it: (1) confirm this pass's edits are still on disk (`git status`
should show `docs/plan/jira-issues.csv`, `orchestration/deps.json`, `orchestration/needs-human.md`,
three task cards and this delta as the only tracked/untracked changes beyond the pre-existing WIP
noted in the trigger brief) before running another cycle; (2) a planner (or any agent) leaving
edits uncommitted in the primary checkout while `loop.sh` runs concurrently there is unsafe by
construction — `cycle.mjs`'s fast-forward step doesn't guard against uncommitted tracked changes
the way `AGENTS.md`'s "do not ff if it would clobber" instruction assumes a human would. Worth its
own story: either the loop should refuse to fast-forward with a dirty tracked tree, or planner
passes need a lock/announcement mechanism separate from hoping nobody's cycle runs mid-edit.

  (`generate_handler!`) and the asset-scope wiring that MARXY-26 escalated on twice before the
  split, plus a CLS-zero Playwright assertion and a new notices subsystem with a hard
  `no-innerHTML` rule. Four separate mechanisms a single story has to get right on one PR
  (Rust command surface, the resolver → box → src ordering, the notices region, two regenerated
  baselines). If it returns once on a narrow miss, that's normal; if it returns on a second
  mechanism, split along the Rust/Tauri wiring vs. the render post-pass line rather than dispatch
  a third narrow fix.
- **MARXY-126.** Nine attempts with zero diff each time is not a story-shape problem the planner
  can fix by rewriting Acceptance — something about dispatching this specific key is failing
  before the implementor writes anything. Escalated rather than retried a tenth time; worth a
  human or the orchestrator looking at *why* (model routing? a card-vs-CSV disagreement it can't
  resolve on its own, given it's the check-cards.mjs story?) before it is ever restarted.
- **MARXY-132.** Low risk on the story itself (registry-check hardening, no product surface), but
  the three burned attempts are an infra signal: headless `cursor-agent` dispatch is failing to
  authenticate again, the same gap `needs-human.md` recorded once before and that was supposedly
  worked around by dispatching in-app. If other `todo` stories were dispatched headlessly in this
  window, they may be burning attempts the same way; worth a `grep -l "Authentication required"
  orchestration/results/*.log` sweep before the next cycle.
