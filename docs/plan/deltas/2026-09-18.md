# Plan delta — 2026-09-18 (first pass)

Board at the time of writing: 45 stories, 0 merges, 0 PRs, 0 implementor results, no notes
files, nothing failed. Previous delta: none. This pass is therefore a correctness pass over
the plan data rather than a re-sequencing of observed work.

## The premise that was wrong, and what is actually broken

The trigger for this pass reported that "only 8 of 41 stories appear in `deps.json`'s graph".
That is not the case. `deps.json` already covered every story: 43 keys in `phases`, all
present in the CSV and in `state.json`, no dangling dependency targets, no cycles. The `8`
comes from `ready.mjs` printing `waitingOnDeps: 5` next to 3 ready — but that counter is
`candidates.length - picked.length`, where `candidates` are stories with **no unmet
dependencies**. It counts lane starvation as a dependency wait. Eight stories are dependency-free
at the start of a project; that is what the number was saying.

Three things *are* wrong, and they are worse than a missing edge:

1. **Path globs collapse to their first literal segment.** `pathsOf` does
   `s.replace(/\*.*$/, '')`, so MARXY-5's `packages/*/src/contracts` and MARXY-8's
   `packages/*/package.json` both become `packages`. Each of those two stories therefore
   collided with 21 others, and holding either lane would have frozen the entire `packages`
   tree — the parser, the theme and the typesetter included. With three lanes, the fleet would
   have run at one.
2. **`phases` is declarative and unread.** `ready.mjs` never looks at it, so phase-2 index
   work (MARXY-34, MARXY-35, deps `[MARXY-14]`) becomes dispatchable the moment byte-faithful
   save merges — during Phase 0, ahead of the typography that the whole plan is sequenced to
   de-risk first.
3. **`review.mjs`'s boundary check forbids `pnpm-lock.yaml`.** Any story that adds a
   dependency — MARXY-11 adds the parser — would have been an automatic *return* for touching
   a file outside its paths.

All three are now either fixed in the data or owned by a new story (MARXY-9).

lands, the orchestrator should set them `blocked`. With those two blocked, `ready.mjs` returns
MARXY-7, MARXY-9 and MARXY-11 — verified by simulation, state restored.
