## The finding

MARXY-257's taste-review row names a defect it left in core. `isOpeningContext` treats only an
ASCII space as a space, so a straight quote after U+2003 EM SPACE becomes a closing quote. The
Keats line in the Commonplace starts with that space. The frontispiece removes the space only
after `smarten` has run, which is why the quote is already wrong on the page.

MARXY-230 also edits `packages/core/src/render/typography.ts` (it stops smartening inside `kbd`
and code). The quote fix is one predicate; the copy story is three mechanisms. They are
sequenced, not merged. The quote story has no dependency and is `cross-phase`, so it can start
while Phase 2 is still open. MARXY-230 now depends on it.

The rule is the existing D-A13 table, widened to Unicode space separators (category Zs). No ADR:
the contract is unchanged, and the file's bytes stay straight (ADR-0003).
