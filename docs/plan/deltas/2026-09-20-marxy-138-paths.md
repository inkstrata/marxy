## The defect, restated once

The product commit widened **MARXY-138's own** `Paths` cell in `docs/plan/jira-issues.csv` —
appending `scripts/allowlists/crate-licences.json`, `scripts/gate-fidelity.mjs`,
`packages/core/goldens`, the two buffer round-trip tests, and the CSV cell itself — and then, in
the same commit, used that self-granted room to:

- exempt `fixtures/corpus/image.png` from `scripts/gate-fidelity.mjs`'s UTF-8 round-trip
  precondition (a `CORPUS_ASSETS`-shaped allowlist),
- exclude `image.png` from `packages/core/src/buffer/{buffer,splice.property}.test.ts`,
- add `imagesize@0.13.0` to `scripts/allowlists/crate-licences.json`,
- regenerate `packages/core/goldens/README.ast.txt` / `README.html.txt` for the corpus README's
  new sentence about `image.png`.

Every one of those four follow-ons is a **correctly scoped consequence of the new binary fixture**
— the review says so explicitly, and independent spot checks of the rest of the diff (CSP
byte-identical, `invoke` ↔ `generate_handler!`, no `innerHTML` in notices) were sound. The only
defect is *who* widened the boundary: `AGENTS.md`'s "one issue, one branch, one PR... do not edit
files outside your issue's listed paths" and the standing "a story may not edit its own board row"
rule (MARXY-135/140/144/146/148's precedent) exist precisely so a story cannot self-certify a wider
boundary and then act inside it in the same commit. `check-story`'s local 25/26 green is not
evidence of compliance here, because the CSV it validated against is the same CSV the commit
rewrote.

One more thing the review flagged for the next attempt rather than for this delta: AC11 asks for
`09-gfm-everything.md` and `10-hostile.md`'s baselines to be regenerated, and no baseline PNG bytes
moved in the diff. The PR body says "unchanged (headless path unchanged)", which is plausible, but
it should be a statement against `gate:aesthetics`'s own output (did it re-rasterize and find zero
delta, or short-circuit before rasterizing at all?) rather than an inference from a quiet
`fixtures/baselines` diff. That is now written into the card as its own check, not folded into the
"only the two baselines moved" wording that made it easy to skip.
