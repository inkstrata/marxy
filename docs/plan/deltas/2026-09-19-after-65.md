## The second finding: `CHANGELOG.md` in `Paths` was serialising the board

`CHANGELOG.md` is on `scripts/registry.json`'s `extraAllowedPaths`, so every story may touch it
without listing it. Nine open rows listed it anyway, and `overlap()` treats a shared path as
exclusive, so those nine could never run in parallel — with `MARXY-84` picked, **MARXY-78, 117, 119,
120 and 121 were all reported `blockedByPaths`**, and the ops lane looked like one story deep. It was
an artefact of a redundant path. The nine rows are corrected here; MARXY-119, which owns `overlap()`,
gains a criterion that makes the correction unnecessary by construction.
