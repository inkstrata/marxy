# Plan delta — 2026-09-19: the ops lane

Written after a whole-project review (MARXY-106, MARXY-107). Nothing here drops product scope.

## What was wrong

- **Phase 1 could not start.** `ready.mjs` holds phase N+1 while phase N has todo or in-progress
  work, and every open Phase 0 story was process, gate or CI work: sixteen of them, five about the
  loop itself. The page, which is the product's reason to exist, waited behind the fleet's
  improvements to its own machinery.
- **Two stories were deadlocked.** MARXY-26 (Phase 1) depended on MARXY-95 (Phase 2), which cannot
  start until Phase 1 closes, so MARXY-26 and MARXY-31 behind it could never be dispatched.
- **The board lived in uncommitted files.** The CSV, deps.json, task cards, deltas and decisions
  had been edited in the orchestrator's checkout for a day and never committed, so worktrees and CI
  saw a different plan from the one the orchestrator used, and four stories had PRs but no row.

## What changed

- A non-numeric `ops` phase: process, gate, CI and release stories. It runs beside the numbered
  phases, never holds one, and loses a contested path to a phase story. Moved: MARXY-8, 15, 16, 59,
  65, 70, 77, 78, 79, 80, 81, 82, 85, 90, 91, 105, plus the two stories of this review.
- MARXY-15 dropped: MARXY-63 replaced its measurement and the 2026-09-18 ruling removed its budget.
  MARXY-16 no longer depends on it.
- MARXY-95 (browser harness with a memory shell) moved to Phase 1, which also ends the MARXY-26
  deadlock. It still waits on MARXY-94, which is `human-gated` on Ian's shell-api ruling.
- MARXY-20 and MARXY-46 summaries now say what ADR-0024 decided: dark is primary, light is the
  second variant. `docs/scope.md` cuts light first, not dark.
- `state.json` and `needs-human.md` are gitignored; the board spec changes only through a PR.

## Sequence this produces

MARXY-75 (provenance in the DOM) is ready now, then MARXY-20 (the theme) and MARXY-21 (fonts), then
MARXY-23 (Knuth–Plass), MARXY-25 (headless render) and MARXY-30 (screenshot baselines). MARXY-94
is the one decision that unblocks MARXY-95 and, through it, images, selection and the palette view.

## Risks that moved

- The fleet's first product-facing work will be reviewed on taste only at the phase gate. The
  headless render (MARXY-25) and screenshot gate (MARXY-30) are what let an agent see its own
  output, so they should not slip behind anything in the ops lane.
- Open PRs that edit the CSV or deps.json (#41, #47) will need a rebase after this lands.
