HARBOUR MASTER'S OFFICE — NOTE TO MASTERS AND PILOTS
Issue 14, dated 3 March 1998

“All vessels must report to the Harbour Master before entering the Lock Gate.”
This is not a request – it is a standing order.

1. Pilotage and dues
   • Pilotage for vessels over 40 m is €85 or £60, payable at the office.
   • A ‘special tide’ surcharge of £12½ applies at springs.
   • Dues are quoted per metre of overall length… see the tariff board.

2. Tide times (café clock, at the Ferry Steps)
   High water   06:12   4.9 m
   Low water    12:31   0.6 m
   High water   18:40   5.1 m
   Heights are above chart datum; add 0.2° of slack for the swell.

3. Notices
   • The Sand Spit beacon is out — keep well clear, especially after dark.
   • Senor J. Peña of the Bilbao packet will be pilot for the next two weeks.
   • The Fish Quay ice plant (Frostline™ units) will be shut Sunday.

‘Ebb and flood wait for no one,’ as the saying goes.

Signed,
R. Alderton, Harbour Master
(Telephone: the office, extension 14 — office hours only)
