Harbour Notice 41/2026 · issued by the Harbour Office

Entrance channel restrictions, 12 to 16 October

Dredging in the approach channel will narrow the navigable width to 40 metres. Vessels over 60 metres overall must arrange a pilot and enter on the flood only.
