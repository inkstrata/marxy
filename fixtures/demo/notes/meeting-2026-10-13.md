# tidemark sync, 13 October

Attendees: Hannah Reyes, Joel Marsh, me. Thirty-five minutes, on a call.

## Since last time

- 0.4.0 went out on the 9th: the walk honours `.gitignore` again, and headings drop their trailing `#` marks. No regressions reported.
- Two reports of `tidemark watch` keeping a core busy on large folders. The worst is a vault of about 38,000 notes, at four seconds or so per save, because watch re-indexes everything every time.
- Hannah has a prototype on a branch, `incremental`, that skips unchanged files and keeps a journal of what watch has seen.

## Decisions

1. Incremental indexing goes ahead, but as a written spec before any more code. An agent drafts it from Hannah's notes and the branch; I read it and send it round by Wednesday.
2. The index format goes to version 3. 0.5.0 must read version 2 and upgrade it without being asked.
3. It ships off by default in 0.5.0, and is turned on in 0.6.0 only after four weeks without a stale entry reported.
4. Windows paths are not part of this. Joel asks on the tracker whether anyone needs them before 1.0, and we decide at the next sync.

> [!note]- Folded details: the 38,000-note vault
> Numbers from the reporter, on a 2021 laptop, before trying the branch:
>
> - 38,112 Markdown files among 41,950 paths walked, about 310 MB in all
> - a full run takes 4.3 s, and `--stats` puts 3.9 s of it in the parse
> - their editor saves through a temporary file and a rename, so each save is three events, which the debounce turns into one run
> - with autosave every 30 seconds, watch spends about a seventh of its time indexing
>
> Hannah's branch on a copy of the vault: 0.5 s for a run with nothing changed, and 40 ms for one save under watch.
